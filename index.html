Every line, one map, no account needed

Atara reads the same GPS feed the operators use. Pick a line and you will see each bus, its driver, its speed and when it last reported — all without signing in.

Why it works

Built on measured data, not guesses

Arrival times you can trust

Each estimate uses the distance from the bus to you and the speed that bus is actually reporting right now, so a bus stopped at the depot never looks two minutes away.

See it on the map

Every bus is a live marker with its heading, so you can watch it approach instead of staring at a number that may be wrong.

Leave the car at home

When you can see a bus is genuinely close, riding becomes the easy option — and every ride shared is a car that stays off the road.
